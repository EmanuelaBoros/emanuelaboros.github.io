2024

Changes

Some people stayed. Strangely, the people I met recently. That doesn't mean they stayed, though; mostly it's because it has only been a short time. They'll go away too. And

many people left, most of them. Is this maturity? Is this what I was dreaming of when I was young? To meet people who don't give a shit? I guess so, yes, that was it.

There was no family, no great friends who stayed. I somehow blame myself: I didn't have the patience to call and message everyone. Before, it was by pager, and after that it was by… letters. I loved letters. You would write one or two a year, and then you would meet once a year, on some occasion, a holiday or so.

Now they all expect messages once a week, once a day, once a month. Can you please stop? I don't have the mental energy to update everyone on the crap I do, or to ask what crap the others are doing. I am already bothered by all the crap I can watch on Facebook or Instagram or TikTok or LinkedIn or Threads or X or… mail, work mail, personal mail, Slack.

I became a bit more drastic over the years. Not more direct; I've always been direct, a quality that people apparently do not appreciate.

So, not to lose my train of thought… oh. Too late, I lost it.

BunnyIepurila

On the way home I had a guy in my ears who couldn't stop himself from singing, shoes on my feet that many people liked, a black blouse, jeans, and in my head "Burn what you have adored, adore what you have burned". I didn't see the road, but I understood it and got off when I had to.

I had got foam on one shoe and was trying to wipe it off. Suddenly I felt so tired, I opened my arms, let my gaze slip and didn't change my mind. Meanwhile, I was hugging him with longing.

I hadn't slept nearly enough, and I couldn't have said why I had woken up so suddenly. Strong, deep dreams that often go on for days. The cat had missed me quite a lot. Now I couldn't part from him.

Sometimes I don't want to be alone here anymore, and my sleep goes away. Close to home, I saw a rabbit in the window of a second-hand shop. I walked on anyway. Actually, I went back and asked him to sell it to me. It was priced at 19.5 lei.

"If you want to buy it, I'll give it to you for 15 lei."
"Really?"
"Really!"
"I had the building fees on me and look, I'm buying myself a toy."
"Haha," the seller smiled loudly and handed it to me.
"Thank you so much!"

I paid 250 of the 350 lei for the building fees too. That's very good. I took the bunny under my arm and I could feel my cat. Thank you very much!

In drum spre casa aveam in urechi un tip ce nu se putea abtine din cantat, in picioare pantofi placuti de multi, o bluza neagra, pantaloni de blugi si cu gandul la "Arde ceea ce ai adorat, adora ceea ce ai ars". Nu am vazut drumul dar l-am inteles si am coborat cand trebuia.

Ma murdarisem de spuma pe un pantof si incercam sa mi-l sterg. M-am simtit deodata atat de obosita, mi-am desfacut bratele, mi-am lasat privirea sa-mi alunece si nu m-am razgandit. Intre timp, il imbratisam cu dor.

Nu dormisem destul de mult si nici n-as fi putut spune pentru ce ma desteptasem asa de brusc. Vise puternice si adanci, ce au de multe ori continuare pe zile. Motanului ii lipsisem destul de mult. Acum nu ma mai puteam desparti de el.

Cateodata nu mai vreau sa fiu singura aici, imi trece somnul.
Aproape de casa, am vazut un iepure in geamul unui second-hand. Am inaintat totusi. De fapt, m-am intors si l-am rugat sa mi-l vanda. Cantarea de 19,5 lei.

"Daca vrei sa-l cumperi, ti-l dau la 15 lei."
"Da?"
"Da!"
"Aveam banii de asociatie la mine si uitati ca-mi cumpar o jucarie."
"Haha" a zambit zgomotos vanzatorul si mi l-a inmanat.
"Multumesc mult!"

Am platit si la asociatie 250 din 350 de lei. E foarte bine. L-am luat pe iepurila la subrat si-l simteam pe motanul meu. Multumesc foarte mult!

The busAutobuzul

I have a mania for imagining all sorts of situations on public transport.

Everyone who gets on and looks around seems to be organising themselves to surround me, so that at a certain point – a well-established point – I can no longer get out. They plan it over several stops, so that at the second-to-last one they can all pull out their weapons and pin me down.

One more than the other, in coats down to the ground, ready to follow me with their eyes and then carry out their task.

Behind me, another one sits in such a way that he can't be calculated by my ability to notice him.

For a few minutes I forgot where I was supposed to get off.

I look out from under the huge sticker that covered half the bus window, and for 0.4 minutes it had been standing exactly at that stop.

I grab my bag and manage to throw myself out.

I smile. I got away again.

Am mania de a-mi inchipui diverse situatii in mijoacele de transport in comun.

Toti care intra si analizeaza par a se organiza pentru a ma putea inconjura ca la un moment dat - bine stabilit - sa nu mai pot iesi. Planuiesc asta pe parcursul a mai multor statii ca la o penultima statie sa poate scoate toti armele si sa ma imobilizeze.

Care mai de care, cu haine lungi pana-n pamant gata sa ma urmareasca din priviri ca apoi sa-si exercite task-ul.

In spate, un altul se aseaza in asa fel incat sa nu se lase calculat de abilitatea mea de a-l observa.

Pentru cateva minute am uitat unde trebuia sa cobor.

Ma uit pe sub abtibildul imens care acoperea jumatate din geamul autobuzului si de 0.4 minute, acesta stationa exact in statia cu pricina.

Pun mana pe geanta si reusesc sa ma arunc afara.

Zambesc. Am scapat din nou.

FrecklesPistrui

Yesterday I saw a girl with freckles and blue eyes. My mind suddenly fogged over and for a moment I was afraid the excitement could have pushed me into madness. In jeans, a thin jacket, with an air of youthful carelessness..

A mood entirely of the moment, stronger than any other impression.. in which I lost my talent for a somewhat justified sadness.. I had calmed down; I felt that the happiness hidden in me would let me sleep again.

Overwhelmed by a feeling – I don't know exactly how to describe it, but extremely pleasant – I got off the tram and ran home.

I slept that afternoon.

Ieri am vazut o fata cu pistrui si cu ochi albastri. Mintea mi s-a incetosat brusc si pentru o clipa mi-a fost teama ca entuziasmul m-ar fi putut impinge la nebunie. In blugi, cu o geaca subtire, cu o atitudine de tinereasca neglijenta..

O dispozitie cu totul de moment, mai puternica decat orice alta impresie.. in care mi-am pierdut aptitudinile pentru tristetea oarecum indreptatita.. ma linistisem; fericirea ascunsa in mine am simtit ca ma va putea face sa pot dormi din nou.

Coplesita de un sentiment - nu stiu exact sa il descriu, dar extrem de placut - am coborat din tramvai si am fugit spre casa.

Am dormit in dupa-amiaza aia.

About shipsDespre vapoare

Very well. My eyes really hurt. It's the result of tiredness, of constant tension. Let's talk about ships now.

You could say about me (I was still talking about me) that I'm lazy sometimes, but isn't this disgusting flaw exactly what lies at the root of progress? If humanity hadn't had lazy people too, we would still be going everywhere on foot instead of by bike or car.

Anyway, but not anyway, all that's left for me is to invent another system of personal motivation, because laziness is taking me over completely and, worse, it leads to sleeplessness. Thanks to certain people, when I do manage to fall asleep, I'm woken up in barbaric ways.

Thanks once again.

Now it's 6:02, now it's 6:20. In this interval, feeling half a day go by.

I have to work. I keep telling myself, maybe it will stay printed in my subconscious. Or it's an early sign of Alzheimer's.

Even so, I manage to look carefully through the 'I don't know how many' DVDs (I stress this word) of pictures/photographs. I realise that I feel very good when I see a "me" from before 9th grade, or even during 9th grade. Good words bring good deeds. It's clear I couldn't be unsatisfied. Double negation, interesting.

So, in peace and quiet, I wait for this scourge to kindly pass.

I have so much to say, and I blame you for not knowing how to listen. You're always in a hurry.

Foarte bine. Ochii ma dor de-a binelea. E consecinta oboselii, a tensiunii permanente. Sa vorbim acum despre vapoare.

S-ar putea spune despre mine (vorbeam tot de mine) ca sunt lenesa cateodata, insa nu cumva tocmai acest dezgustator defect sta la baza progresului? Daca omenirea n-ar fi avut si lenesi, am fi umblat si acum numai cu piciorul, in locul bicicletelor, automobilelor.

In fine, dar nu in fine, nu-mi ramane decat sa inventez un alt sistem de mobilizare personala, fiindca ma cuprinde de tot lenevia care, mai grav, duce la nesomn. Multumita unora, atunci cand reusesc sa adorm, sunt trezita in moduri barbare.

Multumesc inca o data.

Ba e 6:02, ba e 6:20. In acest rastimp, simtind ca trece jumatate de zi.

Trebuie sa lucrez. Imi tot spun, poate imi ramane intiparit in subconstient. Ori e un semn timpuriu al Alzheimer-ului.

Cu toate astea, reusesc sa ma uit amanuntit printre 'nu stiu cate'-le dvd-uri(pun accentul pe acest cuvant) cu poze/fotografii. Constientizez ca ma simt foarte bine cand vad o "eu" inainte de clasa a 9-a, sau chiar in cadrul clasei a 9-a. Cuvinte bune aduc fapte bune. Este clar ca nu as putea fi nemultumita. Dubla negatie, interesant.

Astfel, in pace si tihna, astept ca aceasta urgie sa binevoiasca a trece.

Cate am de spus si dau vina pe voi ca nu stiti sa ascultati. Va pripiti intotdeauna.

Still!Inca !

In short: I'm a little lost.

I'm almost twenty, so I could try to look at things from above. Only my life right now repeats itself day after day, comfortable and dependent on others, and it keeps me in place. And it isn't only the life's fault: I like comfort, I look for it myself, so I make it stronger with my own hands.

I feel caught as if in a pincer. I wasn't born with it; now and then I feel it loosen a little (like now) and I think that in the end I can break it.

I'm tired, I need to sleep to recover, otherwise I won't get through anything. Just staying on the surface takes all the strength I have.

The same dreams. I want them. I like them. I keep them.

I am now practically what would be called a spoiled/capricious person, although I need nothing but a single room (in which, preferably, I stay alone) and vegetarian food. That isn't possible at the moment, hence "demands".

Good. I'm making plans. Some honest, some selfish, and I already know the honest ones will get watered down along the way. I would call on heaven and earth to help me, but I'll settle for the small, accidental people who can be useful right now. Only a beginning – another one – obviously. For now I stay here, in my grief, but the plans have already started moving and I feel something solid under my feet. And if I'm meant to fall, at least let me not feel the fall – that's what I take care of most –.

I take care. I've left you.

Pe scurt: sunt putin pierduta.

Am aproape douazeci de ani, deci as putea incerca sa privesc lucrurile de sus. Doar ca viata mea de acum se repeta zi de zi, comoda si dependenta de altii, si ma tine pe loc. Si nu e doar vina ei: imi place comoditatea, o caut singura, asa ca o intaresc cu mana mea.

Ma simt prinsa ca intr-un cleste. Nu m-am nascut cu el; din cand in cand il simt slabind putin (ca acum) si cred ca pana la urma il pot rupe.

Sunt obosita, trebuie sa dorm ca sa ma refac, altfel nu ies la capat cu nimic. Doar ca sa raman la suprafata imi trebuie toata puterea pe care o am.

Aceleasi vise. Le vreau. Imi plac. Mi le tin.

Sunt acum practic ceea ce s-ar numi un om rasfatat/capricios, desi nu am nevoie decat de o camera de o singura persoana (in care de preferabil, sa stau singura) si mancare pentru vegetarieni. Asta nu e posibil momentan, deci "pretentii".

Bun. Imi fac planuri. Unele cinstite, altele egoiste, si stiu deja ca cele cinstite or sa se dilueze pe drum. As chema si cerul si pamantul sa ma ajute, dar ma multumesc si cu oamenii mici, intamplatori, care pot fi de folos chiar acum. E doar un inceput - inca unul - evident. Deocamdata stau aici, in jalea mea, dar planurile s-au pus deja in miscare si simt ceva tare sub picioare. Si daca e sa cad, macar sa nu simt caderea - de asta am grija cel mai mult -.

Am grija. V-am lasat.

In silenceIn tacere

My hair gets tangled. Nonsense. It recovered, though, over a night slept here.

A few hours have gathered since I've been around, which left room for boredom, a certain kind of boredom. Ill humour. Either the world changed in an hour and forty minutes, or I no longer have control up my sleeve. An endless interval.

All well and good, but a beer solved nothing. In the five minutes I held out, nervousness and tiredness stepped in again. And I fell silent. I've been silent for eleven hours. Two coffees followed. Two or three; if it isn't one, it's more.

One thing I kept: if something happens once, then it's likely to happen a second time, but if it happens a second time too, then it will certainly happen a third time. Set theory, stated earlier and proven now.

I'll sleep and stay silent until tomorrow morning at 8.

Mi se incalceste parul. Aiurea. Si-a revenit totusi pe parcusul unei nopti dormite aici.

S-au adunat cateva ore de cand sunt prin preajma, ceea ce a lasat loc plictiselii, un anume tip de plictiseala. Indispozitie. Fie ca lumea s-a schimbat in ora si patruzeci de minute, fie ca nu mai am eu controlul in maneca. Un rastimp fara sfarsit.

Voi dormi si voi tace pana maine dimineata la ora 8.

Clean dirty!Curat murdar !

"Clean dirty!" That's how my whole week was. I wait for tomorrow within today!

Since June I've wasted my time. I kept trying to make room for work by sleeping in the afternoon, so I could stay up at night. It didn't work. After midnight my nerves won't let me start on my colours, and if I start anyway, I don't sleep at all and the next day I'm a wreck.

The hours are out of my reach.

Now, every evening, I wash my hands ten times after eating a piece of flatbread, out of boredom, one after another, in the bathroom.

In the evenings I go out and walk the streets for hours, light, as if I had no body, and everything I felt during the day goes through my head again.

I dreamed recently; for thirteen hours and a few minutes.

I'm quitting smoking, you idiots, so I can show you how pathetic you look with a cigarette in your mouth; all at one table, all trying to flick the ash, all missing the ashtray and with nothing to talk about. Equal to zero.

This morning, when I tried to get up, my legs gave way. Hours of sleep, hours of no sleep. I don't understand anymore. My strength isn't enough. Only towards evening do I start to feel human, and that is exactly when I have to go to bed, and I go to bed furious.

The 17th of September, Monday, the first day of school, fortunately/unfortunately, for others. For days I had been walking around with a reproach inside me. On the way I saw them all gathered at the gate, like in an old family photograph, all equally awkward: should they say hello, keep quiet, look at the ground, smile?
Classmates from my old class, teachers, the form teacher, parents, people I had seen only once, people I never paid any attention to. I held all of them to account. Some contradicted me for years, others agreed with me without listening, and I don't know which of the two cost me more.

Could I have grown up somewhere else? No. I grew up right here, in the centre.

Faded by reproach, I pulled away from all of them, put my hands on my forehead, and I sit and wonder: should I have been different? grown up differently? somewhere else?

That was my first day of school.

Petty uncertainty. Existence. Whatever I say out loud means nothing a second later. I no longer know how to explain it to you. Impersonal.

Insomnia and courage. Everything for the better.

I come back, I collect myself. Headaches, but the fight is worth it, or rather, I have no choice.

I have.

"Curat murdar!" Asa mi-a fost toata saptamana. Astept ziua de maine in ziua de azi !

Din iunie incoace mi-am irosit timpul. Am tot incercat sa-mi fac loc de lucru dormind dupa-amiaza, ca sa pot sta treaza noaptea. N-a mers. Dupa miezul noptii nervii nu ma mai lasa sa ma apuc de culori, iar daca ma apuc totusi, nu mai dorm deloc si a doua zi sunt praf.

Orele imi sunt inaccesibile.

Acum, in fiecare seara, ma spal de zece ori pe maini dupa ce mananc o bucata de turta, din plictiseala, una dupa alta, in baie.

Seara ies si merg ore intregi pe strazi, usoara, ca si cum n-as avea corp, si imi trec prin minte toate starile prin care am trecut peste zi.

Am visat de curand; pe parcursul a treisprezece ore si cateva minute.

Ma las de fumat, idiotilor, ca sa va pot arata cat de penibil aratati cu tigara in gura; toti la o masa, toti incercand sa scrumeze, toti nimerind pe langa scrumiera si fara subiect de discutii. Egal cu zero.

Azi-dimineata, cand sa ma ridic din pat, mi s-au taiat picioarele. Ore de somn, ore de nesomn. Nu mai inteleg. Nu-mi mai ajung puterile. Abia spre seara ajung sa ma simt om, si exact atunci trebuie sa ma culc, si ma culc furioasa.

17 septembrie, luni, prima zi de scoala, din fericire/nefericire, pentru altii. Umblam de cateva zile cu un repros in mine. Pe drum i-am vazut pe toti adunati la poarta, ca intr-o fotografie veche de familie, toti la fel de stingheri: sa salute, sa taca, sa se uite in pamant, sa zambeasca?
Colegi din fosta clasa, profesori, diriginta, parinti, oameni pe care i-am vazut o singura data, oameni pe care nu i-am bagat niciodata in seama. I-am pus pe toti la socoteala. Unii m-au contrazis ani la rand, altii mi-au dat dreptate fara sa asculte, si nu stiu care dintre ele m-a costat mai mult.

Puteam sa cresc in alta parte? Nu. Am crescut chiar aici, in centru.

Decolorata de repros, m-am tras de langa toti, mi-am pus mainile pe frunte si stau si ma intreb: ar fi trebuit sa fiu altfel? sa fi crescut altfel? altundeva?

Asa mi-a fost ziua de inceput a scolii.

Nesiguranta marunta. Existenta. Ce spun cu voce tare nu mai inseamna nimic dupa o secunda. Nu mai stiu sa va explic. Impersonal.

Insomnie si curaj. Totul spre bine.

Imi revin, ma reculeg. Dureri de cap, dar merita lupta, sau mai bine zis, n-am incotro.

Am.

WaitingIn asteptare

24 September – waiting for a 26 September

Maybe I've recovered, maybe I've found the shorter way back once more, after days of being alone and letting myself drift. Maybe I'm beginning to know you, you, on whom I've "dumped" all my attention lately – by mistake, out of need – but I promise I will take care of you; and of me – or of me; and of you – .

I apologise.

Today, half asleep, I lost my words and let my nerves run free. Maybe I was lost; this morning's sadness won't come back, I'll be able to resist it. We didn't deserve it, neither me nor you – neither you nor me – .

Not insensitivity at all. The opposite is in excess.

I'm sorry.

Mostly because I don't know how to talk to you, to all of you. There is fog in my head, and as long as I'm alone it doesn't bother me. But to talk to someone you need a thread, some clarity, and I don't have them. Nobody wants to sit with me in the fog.. and even if they did, I still wouldn't know how to clear it. Other people seem to lift it between them without any effort, all by itself.

I apologise.

Tomorrow morning I'll stay in bed until half past eleven.

24 septembrie - in asteptarea unui 26 septembrie

Poate ca mi-am revenit, poate ca am gasit inca o data drumul mai scurt inapoi, dupa zile in care am stat singura si m-am lasat dusa. Poate incep sa te cunosc, pe tine, asupra caruia mi-am 'trantit' toata atentia in ultimul timp - din greseala, din nevoie - dar promit ca voi avea grija de tine; si de mine - sau de mine; si de tine - .

Imi cer iertare.

Astazi, pe jumatate adormita, mi-am pierdut cuvintele si am dat cale libera unor nervi. Poate ca am fost pierduta, tristetea de azi dimineata n-are sa-mi revina, am sa-i pot rezista. Nu meritam, nici eu, nici tu - nici tu, nici eu - .

Nici vorba de insensibilitate. Opusul e in exces.

Imi pare rau.

Mai ales ca nu stiu sa vorbesc cu tine, cu voi toti. In capul meu e ceata, si cat timp sunt singura nu ma deranjeaza. Dar ca sa vorbesti cu cineva iti trebuie un fir, o limpezime, si eu nu le am. Nimeni nu vrea sa stea cu mine in ceata.. si chiar daca ar vrea, eu tot n-as sti s-o risipesc. Altii par s-o ridice intre ei fara niciun efort, de la sine.

Imi cer iertare.

Maine dimineata am sa stau in pat pana la unsprezece si jumatate.

Water, dream and troubleApa, vis si tulburare

A step, another step. The black thistles, dry and sharp, bite into my flesh. The blood runs, trickling down. The sun slams its air down like a murderer savouring every moment of strangling his victim. I raise my head, dizzy from the heat, and I shudder. The soles of my feet burn, the ground is so hot, the earth.
I am half conscious.
The shapes around me distort, mix according to laws unknown to me. My hands look for something to hold on to.
They don't find it and give up. In return, perhaps, a wave of calm floods over me, like the calm before death. Images dear to my soul unroll before my eyes. Cinderella runs in her fine silk dresses to meet her prince, and the Rooster from the story cries out in despair.

"Cock-a-doodle-doo, great boyars,
Give back my purse with two coins!"

And there is Prince Charming, and the golden apples, and me.. I'm hot.
Harap-Alb smiles at me, and then I glimpse Snow White with the seven dwarfs in their welcoming little house.

The forest, ah, the forest! So much beauty gathered in one place!

Here is the old man's daughter! Poor girl.. she reaches the well and, when it asks her, she cleans it, working hard without expecting any reward. On her way back from her long journey, the well is waiting for her with cold, clear, good water..

I'm thirsty..

– Ma-ma.. mama.. water..

I managed to whisper, and I fell unconscious.

– Mummy's little girl, wake up, you had a nightmare..

I open my eyes and..

..and I drink water.

Un pas, inca un pas. Ciulinii negri, uscati si ascutiti musca din carne. Sangele cruge prelingandu-se. Soarele isi tranteste aerul ca unui criminal ce savureaza fiecare moment al strangularii victimei sale. Ridic capul ametit de caldura si ma infior. Talpile-mi ard de fierbinte ce-i pamantul, tarana.
Sunt semiinconstienta.
Formele din jur se distorsioneaza, se amesteca dupa legi necunoscute mie. Mainile cauta un sprijin.
Nu-l gasesc si renunta. In compensatie, poate,ma inunda un val de liniste, comparabila cu cea dinaintea mortii. Imagini dragi sufletului meu mi se deruleaza in fara ochilor. Cenusareasa alearga in rochiile ei de matase fina in intampinarea printului ei si Cocosul din poveste striga disperat.

"Cucuriguu, boieri mari,
Dati punguta cu doi bani !"

Uite-l si pe Fat-Frumos si merele de aur si mie.. mie mi-e cald.
Imi surade Harap-Alb si apoi o zaresc pe Alba ca Zapada cu cei sapte pitici in primitoarea lor casuta.

Padurea, ah, padurea ! Cata frumusete adunata la un loc !

Iat-o pe fata mosului ! Biata fata.. ajunge la fantana si la rugamintea acesteia o curata muncind din greu fara sa astepte rasplata. La intoacerea din lunga-i calatorie, fantana o asteapta cu apa rece, limpede si buna..

Mi-e sete..

- Ma-ma.. mama.. apa..

Am reusit sa ingan si am cazut in inconstienta.

- Fetita mamei, trezeste-te, ai avut un cosmar..

Deschid ochii si..

..si beau apa.
