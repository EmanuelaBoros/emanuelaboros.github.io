Personal texts

Stories stitched together from personal writings, notes, diary pages and short pieces from over the years, some more Kafkaesque than others.

Stories

All stories

Each one can be read on its own, but in theory they follow a chronological order. One may say they are autobiographical allegories.

A storyThe Girl from SadburyA girl falls out of bed and into a country made of everything she ever wrote down. Fifteen short chapters. A storyThe Mistake CollectorA girl finds a crossed-out sentence living in a book, and the creature it became follows her for twenty years. A storyThe Fifth BedA girl in a city between two hills buys a bed that officially does not exist, and learns how hard it is to take up space. A storyThe House of Unfinished MorningsA girl visits a house where the day repeats itself, twice, and finds that part of it can follow her out. A storyThe Kingdom of Borrowed WingsA girl follows the road beyond the well to a kingdom where every creature is free, subject to the conditions of the kingdom.
