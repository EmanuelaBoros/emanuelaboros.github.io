What is OCR, and why does it misread old newspapers?

In plain words · November 2026 · about 3 minutes

OCRscanningold print

OCR is the step that turns a scan into text a computer can search. On old print it makes small mistakes, and those mistakes travel.

A scanned page is a picture. OCR (optical character recognition) is the software that reads it. Press play to follow one line through the process.

Toy numbers for one illustrative line. Real OCR engines give a score for every character in the same way.

What to take from it

OCR is very good on clean modern print and much less certain on historical pages: yellowed paper, ink from the other side, unusual typefaces, tight columns. It always gives an answer, and it does not tell you which letters were close calls. A small slip at this first step is invisible later, which is why so much of my work asks what we can still do with text that is a little wrong.
