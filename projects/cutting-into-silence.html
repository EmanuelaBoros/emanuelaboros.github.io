Cutting into Silence

2014 7 images recent photo collection

Cutting into Silence (2014) is a reaction. Something had been done to me that I had no words for, so everything went into the way I look at the camera. My eyes do the talking. They meet the lens, hold it, turn aside and return, and the attitude shifts from frame to frame: cold, tired, amused, daring you to keep looking. I had been looked at without asking for it. Here I do the looking: I stand in front of the mirror, I hold the camera, and for as long as the series lasts, no one else decides how I am seen. But control is only half of what the photographs show. The mirror clears and then clouds over, and sometimes I can hardly find myself in it. A faint smile slips through, half defiance, half disguise. The title says what the images don’t: silence was the place I was living in, and this was the only way I had of cutting into it.
