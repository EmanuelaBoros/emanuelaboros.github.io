Emanuela Boros Researcher · NLP & document AI

I study how machines read imperfect traces.

My work is on AI for difficult documents: noisy newspapers, OCR and HTR transcripts, handwritten archives, inscriptions, administrative and financial records. I build methods to extract people, places, events and relations from them, and I measure what models miss, invent or flatten along the way.

05 — About

Mostly serious work, with a sense of humour

I care about what AI extracts from documents, and also about what it misses, invents, flattens, or quietly ruins while sounding confident.

I come from both computer science and visual practice, so I tend to look at documents as text, images, layouts and evidence all at once. My PhD was on neural methods for event extraction; since then I have worked on historical newspapers, epigraphy, document fraud, epidemic surveillance and open LLMs.

Outside research: photography, handmade things (some of them sold at Quiet Soup Club), gardening with variable success, mostly non-fantasy books, metal festivals and camping.
